7. Tanuki Decor
Source: https://github.com/skyjay1/Tanuki-Decor
Author: skyjay1
Credits: Art by Skart2000, NeoForge 1.21.1 update by Alex (CrabMods)
License: GNU LGPLv3
Used for: approved intake source for one-by-one Blockbench export planning, plus live Wooden Block Clock assembled from user-exported GLBs
Local files: assets/props/tanuki_decor/wooden_block_clock, data/mod_intake
Note: the live Wooden Block Clock uses the user's Blockbench-exported `wooden_block_clock.glb`, `long_hand.glb`, `short_hand.glb`, and `pendulum.glb`, assembled and animated in Godot against the repo's Minecraft-style world time controller. Broader Tanuki asset intake should still be triaged one by one because the mod contains multipart assets, internal texture reuse, and some references to vanilla Minecraft parents or textures.

8. Macaw's Lights and Lamps
Source: https://modrinth.com/mod/macaws-lights-and-lamps
Author: sketch_macaw
License: All Rights Reserved
Used for: Ceiling Fan imported from the user's Blockbench-exported `ceiling_fan.glb`, derived from the mod's `dark_oak_ceiling_fan_light`
Local files: assets/props/macaws_lights/ceiling_fan
Note: the packaged mod uses duplicate blade geometry plus animated texture frames to fake rotation. The live repo asset is the user's cleaned Blockbench export with `fan_body` and `fan_blades` groups preserved, and the in-game fan now rotates `fan_blades` smoothly in Godot instead of using the mod's fake texture-driven animation.
