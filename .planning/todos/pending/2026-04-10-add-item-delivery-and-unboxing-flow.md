## Problem

Buying and owning items is currently abstract and immediate. A delivery or unboxing presentation could make acquiring decor feel more tactile and satisfying without requiring a full economy system.

## Solution

Add a local acquisition flow where some purchases arrive as boxes, packages, or small delivery props before becoming normal inventory. The first pass should stay lightweight and presentation-focused so it adds charm without slowing down basic building.
