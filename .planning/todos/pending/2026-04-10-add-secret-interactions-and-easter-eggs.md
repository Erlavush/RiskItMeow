## Problem

The prototype can become more memorable through small surprises, but there is no system for hidden reactions or special item combinations. Easter eggs could add charm without requiring large new loops.

## Solution

Add a small layer of curated secrets such as special item combos, hidden reactions, rare ambience shifts, or playful interactions. Keep it sparse and intentional so the game feels handcrafted rather than noisy.
