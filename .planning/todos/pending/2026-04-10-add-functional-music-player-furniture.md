## Problem

The decor catalog can grow visually, but there is still room for more functional mood-setting props. Music-capable furniture would add identity and ambience beyond static placement.

## Solution

Add curated items such as radios, speakers, music boxes, or phonographs that can act as room audio sources. Start with simple toggleable playback or curated track switching rather than a full music management system.
