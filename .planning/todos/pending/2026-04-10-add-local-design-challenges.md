## Problem

The game benefits from open-ended building, but some players will eventually want prompts that encourage new room ideas. There is no structured local challenge loop to guide creative experimentation.

## Solution

Add rotating local design prompts such as reading nooks, rainy study corners, compact bedrooms, or mood-specific layouts. The first pass should be offline and lightweight, using simple challenge text and optional completion tracking rather than complex reward systems.
